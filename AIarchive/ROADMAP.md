# ROADMAP.md

> Project Roadmap
> Product: Luxury Cattery Website & CMS
> Status: Active Development
> Philosophy: Build a premium product step by step without compromising architecture.

---

# Product Vision

The long-term goal is to create the most complete and elegant digital platform for professional cat breeders.

This is not a website.

It is an ecosystem.

Every new module should naturally integrate into the existing architecture.

---

# Development Principles

Every milestone must:

- be production ready
- be fully documented
- respect RULES.md
- preserve architecture
- improve the product
- never introduce technical debt

No milestone should require rewriting previous work.

# Definition of Success

The project succeeds when:

- The breeder is proud to share the website.
- Visitors immediately trust the breeding program.
- The CMS is simple enough for non-technical users.
- The architecture supports years of growth.
- New developers can understand the project quickly.
- Every new feature feels like a natural extension of the platform.

The product should become the benchmark for modern breeder websites.
