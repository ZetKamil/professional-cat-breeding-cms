## 1. Intent & Search Dynamics
- **Primary Search Intent:** Informational / Health. Cat owners want to know the best age for neutering/spaying a Bengal cat, whether it changes their active personality, how much it costs, and how to take care of the cat after surgery.
- **Core Content Gap:** Generic guides often confuse castration (removal of gonads) with sterilization (tubal ligation/vasectomy without hormone suppression). Bengal owners specifically worry that neutering will destroy the Bengal's famous wild coat contrast or playful nature. Our guide dispels myths and gives step-by-step veterinary advice.
