---

## 1. Intent & Search Dynamics
- **Primary Search Intent:** Trust / Commercial Investigation. Buyers don't just buy a kitten; they buy the genetics, health, and temperament passed down by the breeding parents.
- **Core Content Gap:** Generic cattery sites present static lists of names without explaining:
  1. The significance of genetic line selection (HCM/PKD clear, FelV/FIV negative).
  2. How adult Bengally live as family members inside the house (not in outdoor wire cages).
  3. The role of 5-generation pedigree lines and physical coat contrast standards.
