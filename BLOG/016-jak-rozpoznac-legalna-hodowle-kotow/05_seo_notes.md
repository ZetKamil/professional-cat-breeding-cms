## 1. Intent & Search Dynamics
- **Primary Search Intent:** Informational / Commercial Trust. Users want an objective checklist to verify whether a breeder is registered in a legitimate felinological association and operating legally under Polish law (Ustawa o ochronie zwierząt).
- **Core Content Gap:** Generic internet guides often mention "kupuj tylko z rodowodem", but fail to explain:
  1. The trick of "stowarzyszenia kynologiczne" set up after the 2012 amendment to issue fake "rodowody".
  2. How to check genuine genetic health testing (HCM, PKD, FelV/FIV).
  3. The difference between breeding registration and actual home environment care.
  4. Real legal contract requirements (warranty, ownership transfer, neutering clauses).
